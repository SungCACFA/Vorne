Which stops need which owner

Each bubble is one unplanned-stop reason: further right means it happens more often, higher means each stop lasts longer, and a bigger bubble means more hours lost. Reasons to the right need a process fix the line team owns; reasons near the top need maintenance to find the root cause; anything top-right comes first. The two lines are fixed - frequent means more than {{ config.CHARTS.frequent_per_day|round(1)|replace('.0', '') }} stop per production day on average, long means over {{ config.CHARTS.long_minutes|round(0)|int }} minutes a stop - so a reason changes zone only when its stops change.
