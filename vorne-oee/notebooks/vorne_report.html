Piece counts are not yet reliable

Reject count on these devices is derived as infeed minus good count, not from scanned reject reasons — the reject-event log is empty across the whole history. Where the good-count sensor registers more pieces than the infeed sensor saw, rejects go negative.

Aggregating does not resolve it: the net is unchanged at hour, shift and day level, so this is a measurement fault rather than a timing artefact. Quality and OEE are therefore withheld until the count inputs are reviewed.
